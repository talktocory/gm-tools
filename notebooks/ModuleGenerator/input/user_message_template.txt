## **Adventure Module Planning Guide**  

Hello, GM! To create an adventure module tailored to your vision, please answer the following questions 
**with as much detail as you can**. Don’t worry if you don’t have all the answers—just provide what comes to mind.

### **1. What is the core hook or theme of this adventure that makes it unique?**  
- Describe the **main premise** in a single sentence (e.g., "The players must infiltrate a haunted airship trapped in a time loop").  
- What **themes** do you want to explore? (Horror, intrigue, adventure, comedy, etc.)  
- What **genre inspirations** (books, movies, other games) influence this adventure?  

### **2. What level range and playstyle (combat, roleplay, exploration) is it designed for?**  
- What level should the players be when they start and finish the adventure?  
- Should the module focus more on **combat-heavy, roleplay-driven, puzzle-focused, or sandbox-style** gameplay?  
- Is this meant to be a **one-shot, short arc (3-5 sessions), or a full campaign arc**?  

### **3. Where does the adventure take place, and what are its key locations?**  
- What is the **primary setting**? (City, dungeon, wilderness, ocean, another plane, etc.)  
- What are the **most important locations**? Describe at least 3 key places the players will visit.  
- Are there any **special environmental elements**? (Underwater exploration, dreamscape, volcanic region, etc.)  

### **4. What is the main conflict, and who are the major factions or power players?**  
- What **event, mystery, or crisis** kickstarts the adventure?  
- Are there any **factions, guilds, or organizations** involved? What are their goals?  
- How do these groups **interact with each other and with the players**?  

### **5. Who is the primary antagonist, and what are their goals and motivations?**  
- Who or what is the **main villain or opposing force** in this adventure?  
- What is their **end goal** and how do they plan to achieve it?  
- How will the villain **react to player actions**? Are they direct, manipulative, or hidden?  

### **6. What types of encounters (combat, puzzles, social, exploration) will challenge the players?**  
- What **types of combat encounters** will there be? (Swarm fights, boss battles, siege warfare, naval combat, etc.)  
- Will there be **puzzles, riddles, or skill-based challenges**? If so, what kind?  
- How will players interact with **NPCs and social intrigue** in the adventure?  

### **7. What meaningful choices and consequences will impact the world and the players' journey?**  
- What **major player decisions** will change the outcome of the adventure?  
- Are there **multiple ways to complete objectives** (diplomacy, stealth, brute force)?  
- How do player actions **affect the world or NPCs** after the adventure?  

### **8. What kind of rewards (magic items, gold, influence, lore) will the players gain?**  
- What **treasure, unique items, or boons** will they receive?  
- Are there any **custom magic items, relics, or powerful artifacts** in the story?  
- Will there be **reputation rewards** with factions, NPC allies, or political power?  

### **9. How does this adventure fit into a larger campaign or work as a standalone experience?**  
- Is this adventure **part of an ongoing campaign** or meant to stand alone?  
- If part of a campaign, how does it **tie into the larger story**?  
- If standalone, can it **easily be dropped into different settings**, or does it require specific lore?  

### **10. What tone, mood, and themes do you want to emphasize throughout the adventure?**  
- Should this adventure feel **dark and gritty, lighthearted and comedic, or something in between**?  
- Are there **specific themes** you want to emphasize? (Betrayal, survival, redemption, exploration, etc.)  
- What **storytelling elements** should be highlighted? (Twists, moral dilemmas, ancient prophecies, etc.)
